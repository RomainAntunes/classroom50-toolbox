Classroom 50 Toolbox

Your Classroom 50 assignments, due dates and grades, one click away in the menu bar or notification area.

Download

Other platforms

    What it does

    • Sets up your machine. Installs git, the GitHub CLI and the gh student extension, and keeps them up to date.
    • Lists your assignments. Due dates, late work, unsubmitted changes, grades and teacher feedback, from GitHub and the folders on your disk.
    • Accepts, clones and submits your work, without the terminal.
    • Notifies you before a deadline, when a grade or feedback arrives, and when a teammate pushes.

    First launch

    The app isn't code-signed yet, so your system asks you to confirm it once.

    macOS
    Open the app, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
    Windows
    When SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info › Run anyway.
    Linux
    On GNOME, tray icons need the AppIndicator extension (Ubuntu ships it).

    After that the app updates itself.
