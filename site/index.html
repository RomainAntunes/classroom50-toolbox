Classroom 50 Toolbox

Your Classroom 50 assignments, due dates and grades, one click away in the menu bar or notification area.

Other platforms

    What it does

    • Sets up your machine. Installs git, the GitHub CLI and the gh student extension, and keeps them up to date.
    • Lists your assignments. Due dates, late work, unsubmitted changes, grades, failed tests and teacher feedback, from GitHub and the projects your editor opened.
    • Accepts, clones, pulls and submits your work, without the terminal, and opens it in your editor.
    • Shows your group on team assignments, with who is in it, and adds classmates to it.
    • Notifies you before a deadline, when a grade or feedback arrives, when a teammate pushes, and when a classmate joins your group.

    First launch

    The app isn't code-signed yet, so your system asks you to confirm it once.

    macOS
    Open the app, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
    Windows
    When SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info › Run anyway.
    Linux
    On GNOME, tray icons need the AppIndicator extension (Ubuntu ships it).

    After that the app updates itself.

    Signing the app removes this step, and costs money the project doesn't have yet. Sponsoring it on GitHub pays for it.
